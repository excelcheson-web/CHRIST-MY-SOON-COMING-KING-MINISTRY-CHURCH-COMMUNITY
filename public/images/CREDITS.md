
## The ministry logo

`logo.jpg` is the church's own artwork.

**A note on the file.** The version originally supplied had a grey "1 of 1" badge burned
into the top-left corner — a screenshot artifact from a phone gallery, not part of the
logo. It was showing in the site header, the footer, the hero card and the app icon. The
badge sat on plain white with no artwork under it, so it was painted out and the untouched
original kept alongside as `logo-original-with-watermark.jpg`.

If the ministry has the clean original, replace `logo.jpg` with it and regenerate the app
icons in `public/icons` — it will be better than a repaired screenshot.
