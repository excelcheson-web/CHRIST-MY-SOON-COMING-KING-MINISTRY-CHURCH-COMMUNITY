## Replacing these with photos of your own church

This is strongly encouraged — stock photography is a placeholder, and a picture of *your*
congregation will always be better than a picture of somebody else's.

1. Crop to the same proportions (3:2) and save as `<name>-lg.jpg` (1200×800) and
   `<name>-sm.jpg` (480×320).
2. Keep the same file names and everything picks them up. Nothing else to change.
3. Update this file so the next person knows what they are looking at.
4. **Get permission before publishing a photograph in which anybody is recognisable** —
   especially children. A signed consent form for under-18s is not optional in most
   places, and a wide shot from behind avoids the question entirely.
